關於年會 · About the Conference

鳳凰城
年度盛會

2026年台美會計師協會年度大會將在美麗的亞利桑那州鳳凰城舉辦，這也是我們首次來到沙漠之州！本次年會結合了專業研討、會員聯誼，以及豐富的在地體驗。

We are thrilled to bring our 2026 Annual Conference to Phoenix, Arizona — a city of stunning desert landscapes, vibrant culture, and warm hospitality. Join fellow members for professional development, networking, and unforgettable experiences.
